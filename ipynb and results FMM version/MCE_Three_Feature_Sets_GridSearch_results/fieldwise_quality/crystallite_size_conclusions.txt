Crystallite-size-stratified OOF performance was lowest for S2 (27-39 nm; n = 69), with R² = 0.462, MAE = 0.858, RMSE = 1.210, and bias = -0.057 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹. The highest R² was obtained for S1 (5-26 nm; R² = 0.684). Across the quantile intervals, R² increased with median crystallite size (Pearson r = 0.29), whereas MAE increased with median crystallite size (Pearson r = 0.35). These subgroup associations are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal effects of crystallite size.
