Crystallite-size-stratified OOF performance was lowest for S2 (27-39 nm; n = 59), with R² = 0.439, MAE = 0.942, RMSE = 1.247, and bias = -0.070 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹. The highest R² was obtained for S1 (5-27 nm; R² = 0.705). Across the quantile intervals, R² decreased with median crystallite size (Pearson r = -0.27), whereas MAE increased with median crystallite size (Pearson r = 0.46). These subgroup associations are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal effects of crystallite size.
